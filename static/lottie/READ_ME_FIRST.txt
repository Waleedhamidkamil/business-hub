    quote.json   -> Quotations      (a price tag / quotation / discount tag)
    po.json      -> Purchase Orders (a shopping cart / trolley)
    dn.json      -> Delivery Notes  (a delivery truck)
    inv.json     -> Invoices        (an invoice / bill / document)

HOW TO GET EACH ONE (about 10 minutes total):
1. Go to  https://lottiefiles.com
2. Search the icon (e.g. "delivery truck"), and turn ON the "Free" filter.
3. Open one you like.  TIP for a clean, professional look:
      - simple single-icon motion (not a busy scene)
      - transparent background
      - loops smoothly OR has a short clean play
4. Click  Download  ->  choose  "Lottie JSON"   (the .json option).
      IMPORTANT: pick "Lottie JSON", NOT "dotLottie" (.lottie) - I need .json.
5. Rename the downloaded file to the matching name above and put it here.
