# Business Hub Cinematic Showcase Design

## Purpose

Replace the first presentation with a complete premium redesign that demonstrates Business Hub as a serious commercial operations product. The deck must work for business buyers, employers, and professional partners. It must feel like a product launch presentation, not a collection of marketing cards.

## Deliverables

1. A new 14-slide static presentation in PowerPoint and Canva.
2. A separate animated Canva presentation based on the approved static master.
3. A separate animated PowerPoint presentation for offline playback.
4. A 14-page PDF.
5. Fourteen 3840 by 2160 PNG exports.
6. Presentation notes and suggested LinkedIn carousel copy.

The previous presentation remains unchanged as a backup.

## Visual Direction

The direction is Cinematic Product Showcase.

- Deep navy background with white typography.
- Teal is the primary accent and amber is the secondary accent.
- Bahnschrift is the presentation font because it is installed, readable, and appropriate for a technical desktop product.
- Screenshots are the primary evidence and must dominate the slide.
- A slim editorial rail carries the slide title and one supporting idea.
- The deck avoids repeated half-slide marketing card layouts.
- Decorative shapes remain minimal.
- Status is communicated with words and icons, never with color alone.

## Screenshot Treatment

The source interface captures are 4800 by 3000 pixels with a 16:10 aspect ratio.

- Every primary interface screenshot is shown in full using contain fitting.
- No primary interface screenshot may be cropped.
- The screenshot must retain its top navigation, side panels, totals, and visible context.
- Screenshot frames use a subtle outline and shadow only.
- Any optional detail enlargement is secondary and cannot replace the full screenshot.
- PDF pages are also shown in full without clipping.

7. **Connected invoice context**

10. **Healthy Master Data**

## Copy Rules

- Every slide contains the exact visible disclosure `DEMO DATA`.
- Never use em dashes.
- Titles are concise and direct.
- Each slide uses no more than one supporting sentence.
- Evidence labels use concrete nouns or numbers.
- All companies, clients, document values, and item records are described as fictional or demo data when context requires it.

## Static Version

The static version is the master design. It is optimised for LinkedIn carousel use, PDF sharing, portfolio screenshots, and review in Canva.

## Animated Version

The animated edition is a separate copy of the approved static master.

- Use a restrained fade or dissolve page transition.
- Use one entrance treatment for the title rail.
- Use one subtle reveal treatment for the screenshot.
- Apply animations consistently across the deck.
- Avoid bounce, spin, typewriter, neon, or novelty effects.
- Motion must not interfere with screenshot legibility.
- The PowerPoint animated edition must play offline.
- The Canva animated edition must be created in the signed-in Canva editor because the Canva editing API does not expose animation controls.

## Accessibility

- Use strong light-on-dark contrast.
- Avoid red and green as an unlabeled pair.
- Preserve written status labels visible inside the screenshots.
- Maintain a minimum body size equivalent to 18 points.
- Put screenshot descriptions in speaker notes.

## Verification

- Confirm exactly 14 pages in every format.
- Confirm all interface screenshots use contain fitting with no crop.
- Render and inspect every final PowerPoint slide.
- Confirm every PNG is 3840 by 2160.
- Confirm the PDF contains 14 pages.
- Confirm every slide contains `DEMO DATA`.
- Confirm no em dash exists in presentation copy or notes.
- Run package integrity, layout, font, and first-party import validation.
- Inspect the static and animated Canva designs after creation.

## Out of Scope

- No modification to the Business Hub application or live database.
- No replacement of demo records.
- No deletion or overwrite of the previous presentation.
- No unrelated Canva brand kit changes.
